Good to know

Questions educators often ask.

Don’t see yours? Bring it to our conversation.

Can you tell me exactly what my pension will pay?

Your retirement system is the official source for benefit estimates. What I can do is help you understand your statement and prepare the right questions to ask them.

How do I know when it’s the right time to retire?

That depends on your plan, your finances, and the life you want next. We’ll talk through the factors involved so you can make the decision with confidence.

What happens after I reach out?

David will contact you by your preferred method to set up a relaxed first conversation. There’s no cost and no obligation.

Where can I confirm the current pension rules?

Always start with your official retirement system. Every guide on this site links to the relevant official source.
